## Description (4000 chars max)

---

You already check your stocks too many times a day.

STALK makes every single check worth it.

STALK is the stock portfolio tracker built for people who actually care about their money. Not a brokerage. Not a news feed. A precision instrument for watching your portfolio move — with AI that explains what's happening and why.

**Your portfolio, always alive.**
Add your holdings in seconds. Watch your real-time P&L update as markets move. See your best performers, worst performers, and total allocation at a glance. Pull-to-refresh, or just let it update in the background.

**Daily Brief — your morning market briefing.**
Every morning before markets open, STALK delivers a personalized brief: how your portfolio is positioned for today, what's moving in the market, and what macro events could matter for your holdings. It's like having a trader friend who reads the news for you.

**AI that knows your portfolio.**
Ask STALK's AI anything: "Why is NVDA up today?" "Should I be worried about my TSLA position?" "What's the risk if the Fed hikes rates?" The AI has full context of your holdings and gives answers that are specific to you — not generic finance news.

**For You — the feed that never wastes your time.**
A personalized feed of what's actually moving: earnings beats and misses, analyst upgrades on stocks you follow, insider buying activity, and whale alerts showing where big money is flowing. Every card is relevant to your portfolio or your watchlist.

**Price alerts that interrupt your day for a reason.**
Set price targets above or below for any stock. STALK runs in the background and notifies you the moment a threshold is crossed — not with generic "market news" but with your specific ticker, your specific price, and what it means for your position.

**STALK Pro — for serious investors.**
Unlock unlimited AI conversations, Whale Alerts (real-time options flow), Short Squeeze Radar, advanced price alert controls, and premium app themes. Try Pro free for 7 days.

---

STALK is a portfolio tracking tool only. It does not execute trades, provide financial advice, or connect to brokerage accounts. All data is for informational purposes.

## Screenshot Strategy (Hero Screenshots 1-5)

Screenshots are the #1 conversion driver on the App Store. They need to tell a story in 3 seconds of scroll. Every screen must have a headline that works without reading the UI.

### Screenshot 1 — The Hook
**What to show:** Portfolio hero view with a green day (+$1,247.83, +3.2%). Full portfolio up, all positions in the green.
**Headline overlay:** "Your money, always moving."
**Subtext:** "Real-time portfolio tracking with live P&L."
**Psychology:** Show the aspirational state. Users want to see their portfolio in the green. This is the first thing they see — it must create desire.
**Design note:** Use the indigo hero gradient, white typography. Show 4-5 realistic positions (NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA). Make the dollar gain prominent.

### Screenshot 2 — The AI Feature
**What to show:** AI full chat view. User question: "NVDA is up 6% today — should I take profits?" AI response: a 3-sentence, specific, insightful answer that references their actual position size (150 shares, avg cost $97).
**Headline overlay:** "Ask anything about your portfolio."
**Subtext:** "AI that knows your holdings."
**Psychology:** AI chat is the most differentiated feature and the primary pro upgrade driver. It needs its own screenshot. The question shown must be something a real investor would ask — not a demo question.

### Screenshot 3 — The Daily Brief
**What to show:** Daily Brief header with the gradient hero, showing today's macro summary, portfolio positioning, and one earnings alert card.
**Headline overlay:** "Your morning briefing. In 60 seconds."
**Subtext:** "Personalized to your portfolio, every trading day."
**Psychology:** Sells the daily ritual. This is what makes STALK a habit, not a utility. The screenshot should feel like a newspaper front page — authoritative, daily, personal.

### Screenshot 4 — For You / Social Feed
**What to show:** For You tab showing the "Hot on STALK" section with real-looking tickers (NVDA +4.1%, SMCI +7.3%) and a Whale Alert locked card with a hint of the content behind it.
**Headline overlay:** "See where the smart money is moving."
**Subtext:** "Analyst moves, insider buys, whale alerts."
**Psychology:** FOMO. The locked Whale Alert card should be visible but blurred — showing the lock creates curiosity and plants the seed for the pro upgrade.

### Screenshot 5 — Price Alerts
**What to show:** Portfolio view with bell icons active on NVDA and TSLA, and a notification banner at the top: "NVDA hit your target. Now $147.23 (+4.2%). Tap to check."
**Headline overlay:** "Never miss a move."
**Subtext:** "Custom price alerts for every position."
**Psychology:** Addresses the core anxiety of stock investors — fear of missing a move while away from the app. This screenshot sells peace of mind. It also shows the notification, which reinforces that STALK works for you even when you're not in it.
