## Revenue Model (From Rex's Analysis)

**Conservative scenario** (3% conversion, 6% monthly churn):
| Month | MAU | Paid | Net MRR | Affiliate | Total |
|-------|-----|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 200 | 6 | $29 | $50 | $79 |
| 3 | 1,200 | 36 | $176 | $200 | $376 |
| 6 | 8,000 | 240 | $1,176 | $800 | $1,976 |

**Optimistic scenario** (5% conversion, 4% monthly churn, strong launch):
| Month | MAU | Paid | Net MRR | Affiliate | Total |
|-------|-----|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 500 | 25 | $123 | $150 | $273 |
| 3 | 4,000 | 200 | $980 | $600 | $1,580 |
| 6 | 25,000 | 1,250 | $6,125 | $2,000 | $8,125 |

$5K MRR (ramen profitable) requires approximately 15,000-17,000 MAU at 3% conversion, or 10,000 MAU at 5% conversion. The Month-3 churn rate is the single most important metric — everything else is noise until that number is known.
