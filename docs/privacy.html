Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

STALK collects zero personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

What We Collect

Nothing. STALK does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data — your portfolio positions, settings, name, and preferences — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (UserDefaults / on-device only).

Stock Price Data

STALK fetches real-time stock quotes from Yahoo Finance's public API. These requests are made directly from your device to Yahoo Finance. STALK does not log, proxy, or store any of these requests. The only data sent is the stock ticker symbol (e.g., "AAPL") as a URL parameter — no user identity is attached.

Analytics & Tracking

STALK does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking libraries. There are no cookies, no fingerprinting, and no cross-app tracking.

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, STALK uses Apple's local notification system (UNUserNotificationCenter) to schedule alerts entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to STALK servers. Apple's standard notification infrastructure handles delivery.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your iPhone using iOS standard mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the "Export to CSV" feature, which opens the iOS share sheet so you can save or send the file yourself.

Third-Party Services

STALK integrates with the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

• Yahoo Finance — stock quote data (Yahoo Privacy Policy)

• Apple — device OS, notifications, App Store (Apple Privacy Policy)

Children's Privacy

STALK is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Since we collect no data at all, there is no specific risk here — but we note it for completeness.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (e.g., if we add a backend in a future version), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. We will notify users via an in-app notice.
